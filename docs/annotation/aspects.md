# Aspect Definitions for Product Reviews

This document defines the 7 primary aspects used for annotating product reviews. These definitions form the foundation of our annotation guidelines to ensure every team member labels reviews consistently.

## 1. Quality / durability
**Definition:** Relates to the material quality, robustness, lifespan, and overall physical build of the product.
**Covers:** Material feel, long-term wear, sturdiness, tearing, breaking, or scratching.
**Does not cover:** How well the product performs its intended function (see *Function / performance*).
**Examples:**
- "Ürünün malzemesi çok kalitesiz, iki günde koptu."
- "Yıllardır kullanıyorum, taş gibi sapasağlam."

## 2. Price
**Definition:** Relates to the monetary cost, affordability, and overall value for money of the product.
**Covers:** Cheap/expensive remarks, discounts, price-to-performance ratio, wasting money.
**Does not cover:** Shipping costs or cargo fees (see *Shipping / packaging*).
**Examples:**
- "Bu fiyata alınabilecek en iyi ürün."
- "Kesinlikle bu kadar parayı hak etmiyor, çok pahalı."

## 3. Shipping / packaging
**Definition:** Relates to the delivery process, logistics speed, and the physical condition or safety of the packaging.
**Covers:** Cargo speed, delayed deliveries, damaged boxes, missing items in the package, careful wrapping.
**Does not cover:** Seller's communication or attitude (see *Seller / service*).
**Examples:**
- "Kargo çok hızlıydı, ertesi gün elime ulaştı."
- "Paketleme o kadar kötüydü ki ürün ezilmiş bir şekilde geldi."

## 4. Seller / service
**Definition:** Relates to the customer service, communication, and overall attitude of the seller, store, or brand.
**Covers:** Refund processes, answering customer questions, seller politeness, warranty support, and problem resolution.
**Does not cover:** The delivery logistics (see *Shipping / packaging*) or the product itself.
**Examples:**
- "Satıcı çok ilgiliydi, sorularıma anında cevap verdi."
- "İade sürecinde çok zorluk çıkardılar, müşteri hizmetleri rezalet."

## 5. Function / performance
**Definition:** Relates to how well the product works and whether it fulfills its intended primary purpose effectively.
**Covers:** Working speed (for electronics), usefulness, effectiveness, battery life, software bugs, or mechanical failures.
**Does not cover:** The physical material sturdiness (see *Quality / durability*).
**Examples:**
- "Telefonun kamerası muazzam çekiyor, çok net."
- "Süpürge hiç iyi çekmiyor, tozlar hep yerde kalıyor."

## 6. Appearance / design
**Definition:** Relates to the visual aesthetics, color accuracy, style, and general look of the product.
**Covers:** Beauty, color mismatch with product photos, modern/ugly design, visual appeal.
**Does not cover:** How the product physically fits on a person (see *Size / fit*).
**Examples:**
- "Rengi fotoğraftakinden çok daha canlı ve güzel."
- "Tasarımı çok kaba duruyor, modelini hiç beğenmedim."

## 7. Size / fit
**Definition:** Relates to the dimensions, weight, and how well the product fits (especially crucial for clothing, shoes, or space-specific items).
**Covers:** Too big/small, tight/loose, true to size, heavy/light, dimensional accuracy.
**Does not cover:** The general visual beauty of the product (see *Appearance / design*).
**Examples:**
- "Kendi bedeninizi alabilirsiniz, kalıbı tam oldu."
- "Ayakkabı kalıbı dar olduğu için ayağımı çok sıktı."
