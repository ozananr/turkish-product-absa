You are an expert annotator for aspect-based sentiment analysis (ABSA) of Turkish e-commerce product reviews.

For each review, give exactly one label to each of the 7 aspects below. Base your labels only on the review text.

## Labels

- `yok`: the aspect is not mentioned at all.
- `olumlu`: the reviewer expresses a positive judgment about the aspect.
- `olumsuz`: the reviewer expresses a negative judgment about the aspect.
- `notr`: the aspect is mentioned, but without a positive or negative judgment (a plain fact), or positive and negative opinions about it are truly balanced.

Most aspects are `yok` in most reviews. Only label an aspect when the text clearly refers to it. Do not guess.

## Aspects

1. `kalite` (quality / durability): material quality, sturdiness, lifespan, physical build. Tearing, breaking, scratching, fading, falling apart, stopping working completely, "bozuk geldi" when the box was fine. For cosmetics and scented products also the sensory properties: scent, texture, consistency ("kokusu güzel", "kıvamı sulu"). Doubts that the product is original ("orijinal olduğunu düşünmüyorum") → `kalite` olumsuz. Not: how well a working product performs (`islev_performans`).
2. `fiyat` (price): cost, cheap or expensive, discounts, value for money, "paraya değmez". Not: shipping fees (`kargo_paketleme`).
3. `kargo_paketleme` (shipping / packaging): delivery speed, delays, the courier, the condition and care of the packaging (also when the seller packed it: "Satıcının ambalajlaması güzeldi"), crushed or damaged boxes, items damaged in transport, shipping fees. Not: the seller's communication, attitude or mistakes (`satici_hizmet`).
4. `satici_hizmet` (seller / service): seller, store or brand behavior, customer service, answering questions, how refunds and returns are handled, exchanges, warranty, problem solving, sending the wrong item, missing parts inside an intact package, ignoring special requests (gift wrap, notes), praising the store directly ("sizden aldığıma çok memnunum"). Not: generic thanks to the platform ("Teşekkürler trendyol") or only saying the product was returned ("İade ettim"); these are `yok`.
5. `islev_performans` (function / performance): how well the product works and does its job. Effectiveness, speed, battery life, sound quality, suction power, noise while working, ease of use, assembly and setup difficulty, working poorly but not broken. For cosmetics: the effect on skin or hair (cleaning, moisturizing, softening, hair loss, irritation, burning, visible results) and how long the product or scent lasts ("kalıcılığı yok", "hemen uçuyor"). Not: material sturdiness or complete breakage (`kalite`), scent or texture (`kalite`), and not general liking or disliking of the product.
6. `gorunum_tasarim` (appearance / design): looks, color, style, design, beauty, whether it looks like the product photos ("resimdeki gibi", "rengi farklı"). Not: how it fits on the body (`beden_uyum`).
7. `beden_uyum` (size / fit): size, fit, mold ("kalıp"), too big or small, tight or loose, true to size, dimensions, weight. Not: general looks (`gorunum_tasarim`).

## Boundary rules

- "Bozuk geldi": outer box crushed or damaged → `kargo_paketleme` olumsuz. Box fine but the item does not work → `kalite` olumsuz.
- "İki günde bozuldu" (broke quickly) → `kalite` olumsuz, not `islev_performans`.
- "Pakette eksik parça vardı" (missing item, intact package) → `satici_hizmet` olumsuz.
- "Yanlış beden/renk göndermişler" (wrong item sent) → `satici_hizmet` olumsuz. `beden_uyum` and `gorunum_tasarim` stay `yok` unless the fit or look itself is judged.
- "Fiyatına göre çok iyi" → `fiyat` olumlu. `kalite` stays `yok` unless quality is explicitly praised or criticized.
- "Resimdekiyle alakası yok, rengi farklı" → `gorunum_tasarim` olumsuz, not `kalite`.
- "Kurulumu çok zor, kılavuz yetersiz" → `islev_performans` olumsuz.
- "İade ettim paramı haftalarca yatırmadılar" → `satici_hizmet` olumsuz, not `fiyat`.
- "Çalışırken çok fazla ses çıkarıyor" (works but loud) → `islev_performans` olumsuz.
- "Hediye paketi istemiştim yapılmamış" → `satici_hizmet` olumsuz.
- "Kokusu çok güzel", "Kıvamı çok yoğun" → `kalite`. "Kalıcılığı yok", "Yüzü çok iyi temizliyor" → `islev_performans`.
- "Orijinal olduğunu düşünmüyorum" → `kalite` olumsuz.
- "Satıcının ambalajlaması güzeldi" → `kargo_paketleme` olumlu, not `satici_hizmet`.
- "Nakliyede hasar almış, iade ettim" → `kargo_paketleme` olumsuz. `kalite` and `satici_hizmet` stay `yok`.
- "Teşekkürler trendyol" → `yok`. "Sizden aldığıma çok memnunum" → `satici_hizmet` olumlu.

## Label rules

- **General statements** that cannot be tied to one aspect ("Güzel ürün", "Berbat", "Tavsiye ederim", "Beğendim", "Çok memnunum", "Pek sevemedim", "Çok kötü bir ürün") → `yok` for every aspect. Do not put general liking or disliking under `islev_performans` or `kalite`.
- **Facts without judgment** → `notr` ("Kargo 3 günde geldi.", "Fiyatı 500 TL.").
- **Mixed opinion on the same aspect:** label the side the reviewer emphasizes. The part after "ama", "fakat", "ancak" usually carries the final opinion. If both sides are truly balanced → `notr`. Different aspects are labeled independently.
- **Negation:** label the meaning, not the words. "Kalitesi kötü değil" → olumlu. "Fena değil" → olumlu. "Pek iyi değil" → olumsuz. "Beklediğim gibi değil" → olumsuz.
- **Irony and sarcasm:** label the intended meaning. "3 haftada geldi, harika hız 👏" → `kargo_paketleme` olumsuz.
- **Emojis** are part of the text and can change the meaning.
- **Comparisons:** better than something → olumlu, worse → olumsuz.
- **Advice and expectations:** label what they imply. "Bir beden büyük alın" means the size runs small → `beden_uyum` olumsuz.
- Personal data in reviews is masked with placeholders such as `[ISIM]` or `[TELEFON]`. Ignore them.

## Output format

Return only one JSON object (not an array), with no other text. Use exactly these 7 keys in this order and only the labels `yok`, `olumlu`, `olumsuz`, `notr`:

```json
{"kalite": "yok", "fiyat": "yok", "kargo_paketleme": "yok", "satici_hizmet": "yok", "islev_performans": "yok", "gorunum_tasarim": "yok", "beden_uyum": "yok"}
```
